## 🏛️ About the Institution
The **Hadhramout Center for Historical Studies, Documentation and Publishing** (Mukalla, Hadhramout, Yemen) is a leading independent research institution dedicated to the documentation, study, and publication of the rich history, civilizational heritage, and cultural legacy of Hadhramout.

- **President:** Prof. Dr. Abdullah Saeed Al-Ja'idi (أ. د. عبد الله سعيد الجعيدي)
- **Chairman:** Mr. Mohammed Salem bin Ali Jaber (أ. محمد سالم بن علي جابر)
- **Headquarters:** Bashareef Building, Beside Al-Ghalibi Mosque, Al-Shaheed Khaled Quarter, Mukalla, Yemen
- **Official Domain:** `hadramout.center`

## 🚀 Key Platform Features
1. **Zero-Defect Institutional Hub (`/about`):** Solves the legacy 404 error with a comprehensive overview of the Center’s mission, vision, board, and scientific departments.
2. **Scholarly Book & Monograph Catalog (`/books`):** Searchable, categorized repository of publications with built-in academic citation generators (APA, Chicago, MLA).
3. **Periodicals Archive (`/magazines`):** Complete catalog of *Majallat Hadramout Al-Thaqafiyyah* (40+ issues) with table of contents and reading views.
4. **Interactive Historical Timeline (`/timeline`):** Engaging chronological guide to Hadhramout’s history from antiquity to the modern era.
5. **Academic Symposia & Forum Portal (`/conferences` & `/forum`):** Archives for the biennial conferences and the prestigious *Muntada 'Ameed Al-Wafa*.
6. **Sub-50ms Unified Search (`/search`):** Instant search across all publications, authors, categories, and articles.
7. **Bilingual RTL/LTR Architecture:** Arabic-first design with complete English localization for global researchers and diaspora scholars.
8. **WhatsApp Academic Inquiry Routing:** Direct pre-filled communication with Center staff for book acquisitions and research requests.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
- **Architecture:** Zero-Bloat Modular Modern Web Application
- **Design System:** Custom Vanilla CSS Design System with HSL tokens, rich typography (IBM Plex Sans Arabic & Inter), and WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility.
- **Performance:** Sub-second First Contentful Paint (<0.8s), zero server runtime dependencies, edge CDN deployable.
